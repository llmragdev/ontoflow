# Governance

[한국어](GOVERNANCE.md) | English

OntoFlow welcomes public contribution, but the official brand and commercial operations are managed by the project owner.

## Principles

```text
Contributions are publicly credited.
The official repository, brand, and releases are managed by the project owner.
Commercial products, enterprise delivery, paid services, and private modules are managed by the project owner.
Unless separately agreed in writing, contribution does not imply equity, employment, revenue sharing, trademark rights, or commercial licensing rights.
```

## Participant Roles

| Role | Meaning |
|---|---|
| Public Contributor | Someone who participates through Issues, Discussions, PRs, or documentation improvements |
| Early Contributor | Someone who makes meaningful early design, review, or outreach contributions |
| Core Contributor | Someone invited to the private lab after repeated and verifiable contribution |
| Project Owner | The steward of the official repository, brand, releases, and commercial operations |

## Credit

Contributors may be credited in README files, contributor lists, release notes, presentations, or other project materials.

Credit does not automatically create:

```text
co-ownership
trademark rights
commercial licensing rights
enterprise delivery rights
equity
employment
revenue sharing
final decision rights
```

| Area | Default Direction |

Contributions submitted to the public repository may be used under the repository license and contribution policy.

## Private Lab

`ontoflow-lab` is a private workspace for drafts, deeper review, and release preparation.

Private lab access is an invite-based trust permission. It does not automatically grant commercial ownership, trademark rights, revenue sharing, or final release authority.

## Legal Note

This document describes project operating principles. Licensing, trademark, CLA, and commercial contract terms should be reviewed legally before code contribution or commercialization begins.
